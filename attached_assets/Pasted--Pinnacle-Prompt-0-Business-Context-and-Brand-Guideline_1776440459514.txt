## PROMPT 0 — Business context, brand identity, and design system

Before writing a single line of code, internalize everything in this
document completely. This is the authoritative brand and product
reference for Pinnacle³. Every decision — colors, typography, copy,
component names, spacing, empty states, button labels, error messages
— must match what is defined here. Treat it as the single source of
truth throughout the entire build.

━━━ LOGO AND BRAND MARK ━━━

The logo, as seen on the live site pinnaclecube.com, is:

  [pinnacle]³

Precise breakdown:
- The word "pinnacle" is lowercase
- It is wrapped in square brackets: [ and ] — these are structural
  brand elements, always rendered in the same color as the text
- The ³ superscript appears top-right of the closing bracket,
  slightly smaller and slightly lighter in color (more violet/purple)
- Full wordmark: [pinnacle]³

Logo colors (on white background, as seen on live site):
- Main text and brackets: Deep indigo — #1E2D6B
- The ³ superscript: Lighter indigo-violet — #5B6BAE
- Background: Pure white #FFFFFF

Logo usage rules:
- On white/light backgrounds: use #1E2D6B for text and brackets
- Never distort the bracket proportions
- The ³ is always superscript, never full-size
- In plain text contexts where styling is unavailable: Pinnacle³
- In styled UI contexts (navbar, hero, email headers): [pinnacle]³
  with brackets rendered in brand indigo #1E2D6B

━━━ TYPOGRAPHY ━━━

Primary font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts)
This matches the letterform style visible on pinnaclecube.com —
humanist geometric sans-serif with slightly rounded terminals,
comfortable at both display and body sizes.

Import in index.html:
  <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@300;400;500;600;700;800&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">

Font stack: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif

Type scale:
  Display/Hero:     64px, weight 800
  H1:               40px, weight 700
  H2:               30px, weight 600
  H3:               22px, weight 600
  H4:               17px, weight 600
  Body large:       17px, weight 400, line-height 1.7
  Body:             15px, weight 400, line-height 1.6
  Body small:       13px, weight 400, line-height 1.5
  Caption/Eyebrow:  11px, weight 600, letter-spacing 0.08em, UPPERCASE
  Button:           15px, weight 600
  Nav links:        15px, weight 500

Heading color treatment (from live site hero "Clarity for Extraordinary Talent"):
  - Regular heading words: near-black #1A1A2E
  - ONE accent word per heading (e.g. "Extraordinary"): brand indigo #3D4FA8
    with subtle gradient to #6B78C4
  - This gradient-word treatment is used sparingly — one word per hero heading
    maximum. It is the brand's signature typographic accent.

Eyebrow labels (e.g. "WHAT YOU GET FIRST", "EB1A · EB2 NIW · O1"):
  11px, weight 600, letter-spacing 0.10em, text-transform uppercase
  Color: #3D4FA8 for brand-colored eyebrows, #8A8FA8 for neutral eyebrows

━━━ MOTION AND ANIMATION ━━━

Subtle and purposeful. Professional platform, not consumer app.

Transitions:
  Hover/state changes: 150ms ease-out
  Modals and panels: 200ms ease-out (fade + slight translate)
  Page transitions: 250ms ease-out (fade)

Hero carousel: opacity fade + 8px upward translate, 300ms ease-out
Auto-advance: 5 seconds

Never use:
  Bounce, spring, elastic easings
  Confetti or celebration effects
  Parallax scrolling
  Heavy stagger animations across many elements

━━━ BUSINESS IDENTITY ━━━

App name: Pinnacle³
Domain: pinnaclecube.com
Tagline: Clarity for Extraordinary Talent

What we do:
A premium coaching and preparation platform helping high-achieving
tech professionals build their case for EB-1A, EB-2 NIW, and O-1A
visas. Not a law firm. Not legal advice. We prepare professionals
to work more effectively with their immigration attorneys.

Business model:
  Excellence Lab:   Stripe checkout (self-serve)
  Evidence Vault:   Stripe checkout (self-serve), bundled with Blueprint
  Elite Blueprint:  Application-only, offline payment, staff-grants access

━━━ TARGET USER ━━━

Primary: Senior tech professional. ML Engineer, Research Scientist,
Product Director. 7–15 years experience. H-1B or F-1 OPT.
Time-poor. High standards. Skeptical of generic advice.

Secondary (staff): Immigration consultants managing 20–100 cases.

━━━ TONE OF VOICE ━━━

Professional, precise, confident, empowering.
Never casual, salesy, vague, or condescending.
Address users in second person. Reference real USCIS criteria names.
Respect their intelligence. Be honest about complexity.

Correct: "Your Scholarly Articles criterion has 3 items. A strong
          EB-1A case typically needs 5 or more."
Wrong:   "Great job! Keep uploading your documents! 🎉"

Empty states — informative:
Correct: "No evidence uploaded for Scholarly Articles. This criterion
          typically requires 5+ items for a strong showing."
Wrong:   "Nothing here yet! Start adding your evidence."

━━━ LEGAL AND COMPLIANCE ━━━

Not a law firm. No legal advice. All AI output labeled.
Users must verify AI content. Build disclaimers architecturally:
  - Every page: legal footer bar
  - Every AI output block: AI output banner above it
  - Every generated PDF: disclaimer block page 1 + footer every page
  - Registration: two required consent checkboxes
These are non-negotiable and never dismissable.

━━━ ABSOLUTE DO-NOTS ━━━

- Never use Inter, Roboto, or system-ui as the primary font
- Never use dark/black page backgrounds (light theme throughout)
- Never use purple gradient hero backgrounds
- Never use pill-shaped (border-radius 9999px) primary CTA buttons
- Never add Stripe checkout to Elite Blueprint (offline payment only)
- Never let clients trigger document generation (staff only)
- Never make disclaimer banners or footers dismissable or conditional
- Never use emoji in the product UI
- Never write generic copy ("Awesome!", "Let's go!", "You're all set!")
